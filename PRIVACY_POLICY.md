# MindTrack privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026. Hosted copy: public/privacy.html (see the README for how to publish it)._

## What MindTrack collects

• Your account: email address and name, from email sign-up or from your Google account if you sign in with Google.
• Your check-ins: mood, stress and energy ratings (1 to 5), the date, tags you pick, and any journal note you write.
• Your companion chats: messages you send to the companion and its replies.
• Crash reports: device model, Android version, app version and technical error details when the app fails. They never include your notes or messages.
• Settings kept on your device: reminder times, theme, language, app lock and intro preferences.

## How it is used

Your check-ins are used to show your history, streaks, weekly summary and insights. Crash reports are used only to find and fix bugs. Nothing is used for advertising and nothing is sold.

## Where it is stored

Check-ins are saved on your device and synced to your private account in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access rules allow only you to read or change your own data. Companion chat history and companion memory stay on your device only and are deleted automatically after 7 days.

## AI companion and suggestions

Messages you type in the companion are sent to Google's Gemini model to produce a reply. To make replies relevant, your recent mood, stress and energy ratings and tags are included as context. Your journal notes are not sent to the model.

This app currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for the free tier, content sent to the model may be used by Google to improve its products. Please do not share anything in the companion that you would not want handled that way.

## Not medical advice

MindTrack is a wellbeing tool. It is not a diagnosis, treatment or emergency service, and it cannot contact anyone for you. If you may be in danger, contact your local emergency number or one of the helplines in the Resource centre.

## Your choices

• Edit or delete any entry from History.
• Export your check-ins as a PDF from History.
• Clear the companion's chat and memory at any time with New chat or from Profile.
• Turn crash reports, the daily reminders and the app lock on or off in Profile.
• Delete your account and all cloud data from Profile at any time. This cannot be undone.

## Children

MindTrack is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the app will ask you to review it again.
